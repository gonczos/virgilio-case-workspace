How to read this navigator

Citius / CITIUS ID is taken from the workbook’s actual CITIUS ID field. REF.ª CITIUS is the electronic filing reference and is kept as a different identifier. Court/administrative acts may have a court reference but no REF.ª CITIUS.

Clique em qualquer ato da cronologia ou nó do mapa para abrir os respetivos detalhes. The drawer shows the procedural stage, actor, status, request/outcome, relationship links and identifiers. Abrir documento original goes to the real Drive document already linked by the project.

The Vista para Advogada / Tramitação Principal view suppresses support/administrative events. Cronologia completa shows everything loaded into this prototype. Apenas recursos isolates events whose stage/threads concern an appeal.

Relationship buttons are navigational: when the linked event is loaded in this preview, clicking it opens that event. Missing linked IDs simply mean that the event is outside the current preview data, not that the underlying event/document is missing.

Ordens & atrasos / Directions & delay is a separate forensic audit of executable judicial directions and MP promotions relevant to implementation, delay, enforcement or case progression. An MP Promoção is not treated as a judicial order, and an outcome not evidenced in the corpus is not treated as non-compliance.

Pedidos / Requests exposes the individual operative requests extracted from Alegações and Requerimentos. Each request keeps the exact source wording, filing reference, later MP/court treatment where linked, and any review flag. A review flag means the treatment chain is not yet fully reconciled; it does not prove that the request was ignored.
