These qualifications fit the upstream proposal. The clauses below replace the earlier replay and finalization wording. They remain proposals pending joint review; no files have been changed.

**Amended clauses**

**1. Collection identity and replay**

A source observation belongs to an identified collection event and its context. An acquisition belongs to an identified retrieval attempt.

Two separate collections may produce identical artifact bytes and still constitute distinct observations. Content hashes identify bytes and may support storage deduplication; **they do not establish replay identity**.

Replay means processing evidence already attributed to the same collection event or acquisition attempt, using its evidence identity and provenance. It creates no additional source event. A fresh collection of identical content preserves the new event, times and context.

If legacy provenance cannot establish whether identical artifacts represent replay or separate collections, preserve that uncertainty rather than silently merging them.

**2. Finalization and later corrections**

Finalization means collection has ended and its known coverage has been declared. A finalized snapshot may be full or partial.

Preserve each finalized snapshot’s original evidence membership and coverage declaration. Later discoveries produce explicit, linked revisions; they do not silently rewrite an earlier delivered state.

| Situation | Proposed representation | Source-event consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **Incorrect coverage assessment** | A revised assessment referencing the existing snapshot and evidence, with reason, supporting basis and supersession lineage. Preserve the original declaration. | No new collection or acquisition. Comparisons may require revised derivations. |
| **Previously omitted artifact from that collection** | An explicit evidence supplement linked to the snapshot, identifying the artifact, its original collection provenance, when it was discovered and why it was omitted. Preserve the earlier membership and identify a revised evidence set and any revised assessment. | May reveal a previously unrepresented historical observation or acquisition; does not claim a newly performed event. |
| **New collection or acquisition** | A new collection event or retrieval attempt in another snapshot, linked to earlier evidence where appropriate. | A genuinely new source event, even if the returned bytes are identical. |

An omitted artifact may be attributed to an earlier collection only when provenance supports that attribution. Otherwise, retain it with unresolved provenance. Discovery time must not substitute for collection time.

Corrections and supplements must identify which evidence/assessment revision subsequent comparisons use. Earlier comparison results remain accessible.

**3. Finalized-only delivery for the first increment**

The proposed first increment exchanges **only finalized snapshots**, together with any subsequent explicit corrections or supplements. Open/provisional snapshot management remains within the scraper. Provisional delivery is optional future work.

This boundary does not prevent faithful preservation or useful incremental delivery, provided:

- The scraper retains individual attempts, times, outcomes and supporting evidence.
- Finalization includes partial or failed collection outcomes, rather than withholding them indefinitely.
- Later finalized snapshots can cover additional scope or new observations.
- Corrections and supplements to previously delivered snapshots remain deliverable.

The tradeoff is delayed visibility while collection remains open, not loss of evidence. **A complete delivery of a finalized partial snapshot is still a complete delivery; it is not a complete collection.**

**Consequential Scraper–VC obligations**

The exchange contract should:

- Distinguish collection-event, acquisition-attempt and evidence identity from content hashes and delivery identity.
- Make replay recognition depend on event-scoped evidence identity and provenance, not byte equality alone.
- Identify the exact snapshot evidence revision, coverage assessment and interpretation revisions delivered.
- Preserve original finalized declarations and accept traceable assessment corrections and evidence supplements without duplicating source events.
- Distinguish original observation times from discovery, correction and delivery times.
- Carry revised comparison lineage when corrections affect earlier results.
- Require finalized status for the first increment, while independently expressing collection coverage and delivery completeness.
- Preserve unresolved legacy provenance rather than manufacturing replay certainty.

**Remaining questions requiring joint agreement**

1. Can the first-increment exchange support evidence supplements and revised coverage assessments alongside finalized snapshots, using the same preservation obligations?
2. What minimum provenance must a supplement provide to establish that an omitted artifact belongs to an earlier collection, and how should unresolved attribution be carried?
3. How will the exchange identify the current evidence/assessment revision and affected comparison revisions while retaining every previously accepted state?

Finalized-only delivery otherwise needs no additional upstream capability to preserve the proposed semantics.
