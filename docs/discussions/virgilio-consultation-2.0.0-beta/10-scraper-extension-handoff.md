The proposed review has four separate decisions per area: what to observe/acquire,
what original evidence to retain, what structured assertions to derive, and what to
expose in consultation. These decisions inform one another but are not interchangeable.
A field omitted from default display can still require preservation. Retaining a raw
value does not establish its interpreted meaning.

A material additional question is binary refresh: how should future collections detect
a new binary served behind the same listing description? The recent selective run
correctly left current bytes of reused historical acquisitions unassessed. Decide a
bounded re-acquisition policy under R4 according to the desired observation coverage
and cost; do not silently treat tuple equality as unchanged content or initiate a full
redownload now. This is a planning gap, not an observed defect in the authorized run.

Recommended next artifact is an updated joint collection-scope matrix covering these
four decisions, existing implementation, required additions, uncertainties and cost.
Use retained results first. Once that scope is selected, R3 defines delivery/dependency
units, R4 supplies interpretation/comparison policies, and R5 defines revision handling.
The accepted semantic foundation remains unchanged.
