## Virgilio evaluation criteria

| Candidate helps answer | Downstream value | Qualification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who is named, and under which label? | Case orientation and searching recorded participant context | No inferred person identity, complete party register or normalized legal role without support |
| What happened or was listed, when and where? | Procedural and document navigation with original dates/references | Preserve the source's date label, raw value and observation scope; do not infer legal effect |
| How do records connect? | Traverse cases, buckets, document occurrences and binaries | Explicit observed relation versus inferred link; preserve direction, inputs and scope |
| What can actually be opened or obtained? | Distinguish listed item, acquisition, placeholder and usable file | Source affordance is not successful retrieval; technical checks are assessments |
| What materially limits this view? | Understand missing context and supported conclusions | No routine operational warning when no evidential consequence |

A candidate needs a concrete use, source basis and explainable semantics; availability
alone is not enough. Richer source context can be preserved without adding a universal
VIEC field. New typing/matching belongs to the integration and attributed adapter rules.
Any additional capture cost must be established or marked unknown, not estimated from
field count. New observations cannot repair missing historical events retroactively.

and new navigation/requests requiring separate investigation. It must also identify
unanswered source questions. The first implementation increment is selected later.
