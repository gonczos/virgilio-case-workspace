required material; a transient location cannot satisfy it. Both first-Citius-profile
drafts require Virgilio custody and validation before broker acceptance. Staging or
adapter success is insufficient.

that later disappears. Required original assertions, supporting artifacts and
derivation material are already retained under Virgilio custody. Previously accepted
dependencies can be reused subject to availability and validation obligations.
